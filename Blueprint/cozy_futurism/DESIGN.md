## Brand & Style

This design system defines an aesthetic termed Cozy Futurism: human-centered, grounded, and unhurried. Designed for an intelligent modern coffeehouse experience, it discards typical technology clichés—such as electric neon accents, glassmorphic blurs, and floating glow effects—in favor of tactile, organic ceramic forms, warm ambient surfaces, and editorial typography.

The target audience seeks both artisanal warmth and seamless daily utility. Interactions evoke the reassuring weight of stoneware, the aroma of roasted beans, and the serene quiet of a sunlit café corner. Visual density is intentionally relaxed, treating negative space as an intentional pause rather than empty canvas.

## Colors

The palette establishes an earthy, roasted atmosphere rooted in natural coffeehouse materials. Deep Espresso Brown (`#352A24`) anchors the brand, functioning as primary typography, deep structural containers, and primary buttons. Warm Cream (`#F7F3EA`) serves as the base canvas, layered subtly with Porcelain (`#FFFCF7`) and Oat Milk (`#F1E9DE`) to denote structural hierarchy.

Three experiential sub-palettes provide immediate contextual distinction without visual chaos:
- **Café Flow** utilizes `flow-bg` (`#E6EDE3`) grounded by deep Forest Ink `flow-text` (`#3C5A45`) for order staging, queue pacing, and ambient status.
- **Café Connect** pairs `connect-bg` (`#F3DFD5`) with high-contrast Dark Clay `connect-text` (`#7E3F2D`) for community interactions, messages, and social gathering.
- **Coffee Circles** employs `circles-bg` (`#F3E6C9`) alongside Honey Ink `circles-text` (`#72531B`) for loyalty, recurring tastings, and subscription tiers.

In dark contexts, the canvas shifts to deep roasted hues (`#241C18` and `#2C231F`) with Warm Cream text, maintaining warmth over cold digital blacks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the warm, expressive character of Fraunces with the crisp utilitarian clarity of DM Sans. 

Fraunces is reserved for moments of editorial character: page titles, key brand statements, and hero value propositions. Its soft serifs and organic curves mirror handcrafted ceramics. DM Sans handles all functional tasks—body prose, metadata, system feedback, and interactive labels. Its geometric neutrality balances the personality of Fraunces, keeping menus and ordering flows effortless to scan.

## Layout & Spacing

A strict 4px modular rhythm underpins the layout. Layouts follow a mobile-first fluid approach that expands into a 12-column grid at desktop viewports. 

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked flows. Outer canvas margins sit at `1rem` (16px) with an edge-to-edge breathing room designed for single-thumb navigation.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 6-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and margins. Cards collapse into paired 3-column modules.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid capped at an optimal reading container width of 1200px. Gutters scale to `2rem` (32px), with generous vertical padding (`3rem` to `4rem`) separating thematic sections.

All primary touch elements strictly preserve an absolute minimum hit target height of 48px to guarantee relaxed, ergonomic interaction.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive floating drop-shadows and artificial blurs, drawing instead from physical ceramic layers sitting upon raw wooden counters. 

Hierarchy is established tonally:
1. **Canvas Level:** Base Warm Cream (`#F7F3EA`) surface.
2. **Resting Containers:** Porcelain (`#FFFCF7`) or Oat Milk (`#F1E9DE`) flats framed by a whisper-fine 1px border (`#D7C7B9`).
3. **Lifted Elements:** Elevated cards and active overlays employ an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted shadow (`0 6px 24px rgba(53, 42, 36, 0.07)`). The shadow color is explicitly derived from the espresso primary token rather than neutral synthetic black, preserving visual warmth.
4. **Dark Mode:** Surfaces stack tonally from Roasted Dark (`#241C18`) to Elevated Roast (`#2C231F`), omitting shadows entirely and demarcating bounds through subtle warm line work (`#725F53`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary takes direct inspiration from pottery, organic ceramics, and smoothed river stones. Sharp 90-degree corners are avoided across all interface elements.

- **Base Radius (12px / 0.75rem):** Used for interactive input containers, system alerts, and notification toasts.
- **Large Radius (18px / 1.125rem):** Standard for content cards, modular feature blocks, and nested panels.
- **Extra Large Radius (24px / 1.5rem):** Applied to raised primary containers and prominent brand displays.
- **Pill Radius (999px):** Applied systematically to all interactive buttons, toggle chips, search inputs, and status badges.

## Components

### Buttons
All buttons share a 48px height and full-pill roundedness.
- **Primary:** Espresso Brown (`#352A24`) fill with Warm Cream (`#F7F3EA`) text. On hover, transitions cleanly to `#4B3930`.
- **Secondary:** Oat Milk (`#F1E9DE`) fill with Espresso Brown text, bordered by a subtle 1px border (`#D7C7B9`).
- **Contextual Variants:** Café Flow buttons utilize Sage (`#56715D`) fill with white text; Café Connect buttons use Terracotta (`#985239`) fill.

### Input Fields
Inputs are set to a 48px height with a 12px corner radius, a clean white surface (`#FFFFFF`), and a 1px border (`#D7C7B9`). Padding is fixed at `12px 16px`. Active focus transitions to a high-contrast Forest Green border (`#3C5A45`) with no outward drop-shadow glow.

### Cards & Modules
- **Standard Card:** Porcelain (`#FFFCF7`) background, 18px radius, 24px internal padding, and a 1px border (`#D7C7B9`).
- **Module Cards:** Café Flow, Connect, and Circles use their respective contextual background fills (`#E6EDE3`, `#F3DFD5`, `#F3E6C9`) paired with their high-contrast ink colors (`#3C5A45`, `#7E3F2D`, `#72531B`) for text and icons.

### Chips & Badges
Chips are styled with full-pill curvature, compact padding (`6px 14px`), and a 12px DM Sans medium label. Resting chips use an Oat Milk fill; selected chips swap to deep Espresso Brown with Warm Cream typography.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
Rounded controls (radio circular, checkbox 6px radius) in an Oat Milk frame. When selected, the control fills with primary Espresso Brown displaying a porcelain-colored check or dot. Focused states project a 2px offset ring in `#3C5A45`.
